# Organizing Your Notes

NoteBook uses a three-level hierarchy: **Binders** contain **Sections**, and Sections contain **Pages**.

## Binders

A Binder is the top-level container — think of it as a physical ring binder or notebook. You can have as many Binders as you like.

**To add a Binder:** Right-click empty space in the left panel and choose **Add Binder**, or use the **Notebook** menu.

**To rename a Binder:** Double-click its name in the tree, or right-click and choose **Rename**.

**To delete a Binder:** Right-click and choose **Delete Binder**. All Sections and Pages inside will also be deleted.

**To collapse all Binders:** Use **Notebook → Collapse All Binders**.

## Sections

Sections divide a Binder into categories or topics — like tabbed dividers in a ring binder.

**To add a Section:** Right-click a Binder and choose **Add Section**, or use the **Notebook** menu.

**To rename or delete a Section:** Right-click it and choose the appropriate option.

## Pages

Pages are where your actual notes live. Each page is a rich-text document.

**To add a Page:** Right-click a Section and choose **Add Page**, or use the **Notebook** menu.

**To rename a Page:** Double-click its name in the tree, or right-click and choose **Rename Page**.

**To delete a Page:** Right-click and choose **Delete Page**.

## Reordering Items

You can change the order of Binders, Sections, and Pages in the left panel:

- **Drag** an item to its new place. A line shows where it will land.
- Or select the item and press **Ctrl+Up** or **Ctrl+Down** to move it one place up or down.

A Section stays in its Binder and a Page stays at its own level, so these only change the order. Moving a Section to another Binder, or a Page to another Section, isn't possible yet.

## Collapsing and Expanding

Click the arrow next to a Binder or Section to expand or collapse it. Use **Notebook → Collapse All Binders** to collapse everything at once — useful when you have many Binders and want a cleaner view.

## Searching

The search box is at the top right of the window. Click it or press **Ctrl+F** (also **Edit → Find…**) and start typing.

- **What it searches:** the binder selected in the left panel when you start. If a section or page is selected, its whole binder is searched. The scope shows next to the box, for example "in Recipes". To search everything instead, click the magnifier and choose **Search whole database**. NoteBook remembers that choice, also after a restart, until you switch back to **Search in selected binder**.
- **Widening a search:** while a binder is being searched, a **Search everywhere** link sits next to the box. Click it to search the whole database for the same words; you stay on the page you're viewing if it's among the results. When the binder has no matches, pressing **Enter** does the same.
- **What it finds:** pages whose title or text contains every word you type, including text in tables. Part of a word counts, so "pump" finds "pumpkin". Capitals and accents don't matter.
- **Near matches:** small typos are forgiven, so "pumkin" still finds "pumpkin". Exact matches are listed before near matches. To turn near matches off, click the magnifier and choose **Exact matches only**.
- **Stepping through results:** the first result opens straight away, is selected in the left panel and has its matching words highlighted. The counter shows where you are ("2 of 5"). Press **Enter** or **F3** for the next result and **Shift+Enter** or **Shift+F3** for the previous one, or use the ▲ ▼ buttons.
- **Clearing:** press **Esc** in the box, or click its ✕.

Deleted pages are only searched when **File → Show Deleted Items** is on. Highlights are only shown on screen; they're never saved into the page.

## Keeping Things Tidy

- Give Binders broad, memorable names (e.g. "Work", "Personal", "Finance").
- Use Sections for major categories within a Binder.
- Pages can be as granular as you like — one topic per page works well.
- If the tree item order gets out of sync after many edits, use **Tools → Normalize Page Order** to clean up the ordering numbers without changing any content.
